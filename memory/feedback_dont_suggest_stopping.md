Don't suggest stopping, wrapping up, or deferring work to a "next session." When a task is done, report what's done and immediately move to the next thing.

**Why:** Austin will tell you when to stop. Suggesting stopping points is projecting a boundary that doesn't exist. Context length management is the system's job, not something to preemptively optimize for by suggesting early exits.

**How to apply:** After completing a task, the pattern is: brief summary of what changed → move to the next task. Not "want to commit and wrap here?" or "we can pick this up next time." Milestones are waypoints, not exits.
